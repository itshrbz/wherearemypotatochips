## Title

Where Are My Potato Chips?

## Short description (the "Short description or tagline" field, ~100 characters max)

> A customer asks for potato chips. The warehouse isn't the warehouse anymore. Something follows you.

## Long description

**Night shift at Potato Mart. Again.**

Same humming lights. Same empty aisles. Customers who never really look at you. Tonight, one of them asks for potato chips. The shelf is empty, but there should be a box in the warehouse. Just go to the back and get it. It will only take a minute.

But behind the door, it isn't your warehouse.

*Where Are My Potato Chips?* is a short first-person liminal horror game. You walk through three places that should be ordinary and aren't: a warehouse of yellow hallways that never end, white-tiled pools lit from below by the water, and a playground at dusk without a single child. Every place ends in an almost perfect copy of your store, where someone is waiting to pay.

And in every place, something follows you. It sees you when you're in front of it and hears you when you run. Break its line of sight, crouch in a hiding spot and wait. If it sees you hide, it knows where you are.

Made in 48 hours for **GameJam+ 2026** under the theme *"Something's Off"*, then polished by the same team.

### Features

- Three liminal places that appear in a random order every run.
- A stalker that hunts you by sight and sound, and remembers where you hide.
- A replica store at the end of each place: almost the same as yours, with one detail that doesn't fit.
- A 90s camcorder look (grain, aberration, lens distortion) that you can tone down or turn off in the options.
- Short runs, about [15–25] minutes.

### Accessibility and content warnings

- Contains flickering lights, chases and sound-based jump scares. No blood or graphic violence.
- Camera sway and the old-video look can be turned off in the options.
- Adjustable mouse sensitivity, invert Y and field of view.
